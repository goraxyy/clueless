Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

What Clueless looks at, and when

  • Your screen, only when you ask: the read-screen and select-area hotkeys or buttons, Ask and Animate on a highlight, and each message you send from the floating panel (so Clueless can see what you're asking about). Messages typed in the main window go without a screenshot.
  • Text you highlight, when the island's highlight feature is on: Clueless copies the selection to read it, then puts your clipboard back.
  • Your voice, only while you hold the talk button.
  • Whether you seem stuck: Clueless takes tiny grayscale thumbnails of your screen and reads the titles of open windows. These stay on your computer and are never sent anywhere.

Where it goes

  • Screenshots and your messages go to the Clueless server and on to Anthropic's Claude, which writes the replies.
  • Voice recordings stream to Deepgram, which turns them into text.
  • Replies read aloud: their text goes to ElevenLabs, which turns it into speech.
  • Each of these providers handles the data under its own terms.

What the Clueless server keeps

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • Your plan, and for Pro your subscription's ID and status.
  • For each request: when it happened, which model or service it used, how much it used (tokens or characters) and what it cost. Not the screenshot, and not what was said.

What stays on your computer

  • Your History (past problems and their conversations), the topics you got stuck on, and your flashcards.
  • Your sign-in, encrypted with your computer's keychain where available.

Payments

Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which handles your payment details. Clueless never sees your card. It's told only whether your subscription is active.

Deleting your data

Delete problems from History in the app, and flashcards from Flashcards. To delete your account and what the server keeps, email YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL.
