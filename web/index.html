Everything a stuck student needs

On macOS and Windows, over any app, even full-screen ones.

Reads your screen

⌘ ⇧ Space reads the problem. ⌘ ⇧ 2 lets you box just the part you mean.

Hints that grow

Hint, What next?, Check my work and Example: each one a little more help, never the whole answer.

Checks your math

Type your answer and a math engine checks it, so you know whether you're right before moving on.

Animate

Highlight code or an idea, and watch it run step by step, with a voice, while the line lights up.

Talk to it

Hold ⌘ ⇧ 1 and ask out loud. Replies can be read back to you.

History and flashcards

Every problem is saved. Keep the explanations that clicked as flashcards and review them later.
