Terms

Beta draft. Last updated November 2026.

Who can use it

During the beta, Clueless is for college students who are 18 or older and have an invite code. By using it you confirm you're 18 or older.

It's a beta

Clueless is free while we test it, with a weekly limit. It may have bugs, change or stop working, and we may change the limits. It's provided as is, without guarantees.

Use it to learn

  • Clueless is built to help you work problems out yourself, not to do your work for you.
  • Follow your school's academic integrity rules. If a class doesn't allow AI help, don't use Clueless for it.
  • AI can be wrong. Check important answers yourself.

Don't misuse it

Don't share your account, get around the limits, use Clueless for anything illegal or try to break or overload the service. We may suspend accounts that do.

Your stuff

Your problems and your work stay yours. We only use them to run Clueless for you, as described in the privacy page.
