Privacy

Beta draft. Last updated November 2026.

Clueless looks at your screen to help you with homework, so here is exactly what it keeps and where things go.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address (or the one from Google), when you joined, your invite code and that you confirmed you're 18 or older.
  • Your history: the text of each problem session (the title, the steps, what you and Clueless wrote, the gaps it noticed) so you can read it back or continue on another computer.
  • Usage: how many problems and questions you used each week and what they cost us, so we can keep the beta free and fair.

What we don't keep

  • Screenshots. Clueless only takes one when you ask it to. It goes straight to the AI model for that request and is never saved, by us or in your history.
  • Your voice. When you talk to Clueless, the audio is turned into text and then thrown away.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Who else handles it

  • Anthropic runs Claude, the AI model behind the hints. It receives the problem, your screenshot when you ask, and the conversation. Anthropic doesn't train its models on this data.
  • Supabase hosts our accounts and database.
  • Deepgram turns your voice into text, and ElevenLabs reads replies aloud, only if you use voice.
  • GitHub hosts the app downloads and updates.

Deleting things

  • Delete one session from History in the app, or everything from Settings → Delete all history. It's deleted from every computer.
  • To delete your whole account, email us at CONTACT_EMAIL and we'll do it within a week.
