Install Clueless

About two minutes. You'll need your invite code.

On a Mac

  1. Download Clueless for Mac. It works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  2. Open the file and drag Clueless into Applications.
  3. Open Clueless from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
  4. Sign in with Google or your email, then enter your invite code.
  5. The first time you press Read my screen, macOS asks to allow Screen Recording. Turn it on in System Settings, then reopen Clueless. Turn on Accessibility too if you want the Ask button when you highlight text, and allow the Microphone if you want to talk to it.

If macOS says it can't check Clueless for malicious software, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. This only happens with early beta builds.

On Windows

  1. Download Clueless for Windows. It works on Windows 10 and 11.
  2. Open the installer. During the beta Windows may show Windows protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer, then open Clueless. Its icon appears in the system tray, next to the clock.
  4. Sign in with Google or your email, then enter your invite code.

Updates

Clueless downloads new versions by itself. When one is ready you'll see Restart to update in the panel, or it installs the next time you quit.

Shortcuts

  • Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Space: read the screen and start helping
  • Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + 2: select just the problem
  • Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + 1: talk to Clueless
  • Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + 3: show or hide the island
